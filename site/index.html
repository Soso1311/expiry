Questions

The ones everyone asks.

We already use Alto / Reapit / Street.

Good — keep it. Those are practice management systems and they are built for the whole agency. This is the compliance check that runs alongside, and it takes a portfolio export to set up. Nobody switches software for a compliance report.

Do we have to move our data?

You send an export, I import it, and the demo stores nothing on a server. If you want the hosted version later, it is your call and your data comes with you.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is a reminder system. You remain responsible for holding valid certificates and serving the right documents before a tenancy starts. Nothing here makes an agency compliant on its own.
